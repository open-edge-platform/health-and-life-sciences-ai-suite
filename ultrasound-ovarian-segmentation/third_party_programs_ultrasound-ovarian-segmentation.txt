This application does NOT redistribute any model weights, model files, or
datasets. The model-preparation step downloads model weights and a dataset
locally and produces an OpenVINO IR on the user's machine; review the

The app offers two model architectures. The default `ds2net` arch uses the
dependencies in sections 2 and 3. The optional `sam` arch additionally uses the
dependencies in sections 2b and the weights in section 3.
